💡 Why Sliding Windows Collapse

When shifting across parameter scales (1B vs 3B) or quantization precision (Q4 vs Q8), instantaneous distribution differences are subtle. Rolling window KS tests without memory achieve only 14%–28% power.

🏆 Adaptive CUSUM Dominates Subtle Shifts

By accumulating persistent small z-score shifts over time, Adaptive CUSUM reaches 92.86% power on Medium and 71.43% on Hard quantization shifts.
